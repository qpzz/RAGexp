Discussion and Future Work  Updating the index.Real-time document insertion and index updates are common requirements for RAG applications, yet they pose challenges for traditional Tree-RAG methods, as adding new documents necessitates re-clustering the entire corpus. Our abstract tree index is constructed based on local pairwise similarities, making it feasible to be updated with new documents.                                                                          30
